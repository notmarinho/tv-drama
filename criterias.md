## Focus

The focus is always on the leftmost series. Scrolling through series horizontally is done by shifting the movie strip, not the focus.

A zone with a reserved seat, the content and poster change according to which series is in focus.

When moving down, the upper section is hidden. The focus is always in its place. The movement occurs only in rows and ribbons

## Player

Home button and next series button

Player in a losing streak

In inactive state - the player panel and buttons disappear in 10 seconds.
With any action from the remote control, the player panel and buttons reappear

On the left is the current time of the series where the cursor is (02:01)
On the right is the total time of the series (4:06)
